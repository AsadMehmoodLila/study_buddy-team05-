# Study Buddy: AI-Powered Academic Assistant

**Developed by:** Abdul Rehman, Adeel Mughal, & Asad  
**Academic Program:** Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (BS AI)  

---

## Executive Summary
Study Buddy is an advanced, Streamlit-powered educational ecosystem engineered to optimize the student learning experience. By seamlessly transforming static academic resources into interactive, structured study materials, the platform empowers students to comprehend complex concepts efficiently. Utilizing state-of-the-art AI, Study Buddy facilitates automated summarization, adaptive assessments, and strategic study planning to maximize academic productivity.

## Key Capabilities

* **Advanced Document Processing & Contextual Chat:** Perform robust local text extraction across multiple formats (PDF, Markdown, TXT, PPTX). Engage in highly contextual document querying utilizing strict grounding to prevent AI hallucinations.
* **Automated Content Synthesis:** Instantly generate comprehensive study notes, active-recall flashcard decks, and adaptive multiple-choice quizzes derived exclusively from user-uploaded data.
* **Algorithmic Smart Sequencing:** Automatically structure multiple documents into a logical, AI-ranked learning roadmap, prioritizing foundational knowledge prior to advanced topics.
* **Integrated Productivity Suite:** Optimize academic workflows via a built-in priority-based task manager and a rigorously timed Pomodoro (25/5) focus module.
* **Data-Driven Progress Analytics:** Monitor educational milestones through dynamic, interactive data visualizations tracking quiz performance, task completion rates, and sustained focus sessions.
